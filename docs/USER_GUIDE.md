# SamadhanPoint User Guide

## Citizen
1. Register or sign in.
2. Use New Complaint.
3. Enter pincode or capture GPS.
5. Run AI Triage and review language/category/priority/SLA/duplicate signals.
6. Submit and save the tracking code.
7. Track status and SLA countdown.
8. Verify a RESOLVED complaint to close it, or submit an appeal after resolution.

## Admin
- Dashboard: city-wide KPIs, SLA radar, telemetry and 24-ward operations.
- Ward Control: live complaint/department/worker/head/SLA details for all database-managed wards.
- User Management: manage non-citizen operational accounts and reset their passwords without exposing plaintext passwords.
- Analytics/Evaluation: metrics and live SLA evidence.
- Audit & Reports: audit trail and PDF/CSV reports.
- Customize Widgets: drag/pin and persist the layout in PostgreSQL.

## Department Head
Only the assigned department + ward scope is available. Assign workers, monitor SLA, review appeals and inspect department reports.

## Worker
Open My Tasks, move assigned complaints through IN_PROGRESS and RESOLVED, and add resolution evidence/notes.

## Auditor
Read-only access to complaints, reports, evaluation evidence and audit logs.
